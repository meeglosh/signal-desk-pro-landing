Press a key. Hear what it does.

Every mix ends up somewhere you didn't mix it: a car, a phone speaker, one earbud. In the app, Signal Desk runs your track through those places. Tap a mode to engage it. Tap it again to A/B against the source.

Sources

Bounce to Dropbox. It's on your phone.

Link one Dropbox folder and Signal Desk keeps it in sync, subfolders included. New bounces come in, revisions are matched to the track they replace, and nothing is overwritten until you approve it. Synced tracks play offline.

Dropbox

A dedicated app folder, kept in sync. An image in a folder becomes that playlist's cover art.

Google Drive and Files

Import from Google Drive, iCloud Drive or anything else in the iOS Files picker. No Google sign-in.

Library · Export

Take any track with you, in the format you need.

Export any track as WAV, AIFF or AAC, then send it through the share sheet to AirDrop, Dropbox or Messages. Playlists get their own cover art from your camera or photo library. Playback continues in the background, with artwork on the lock screen, in Control Center and in CarPlay.
