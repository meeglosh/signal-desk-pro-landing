# Signal Desk Pro: BRIEF

Partly interviewed, partly self-authored under explicit creative delegation (2026-10-17).

## Interviewed answers (verbatim choices)
- Range: **Retro hardware**
- Structure: **Distinct chapters**
- Assets: **Real app screenshots only** (no generation, no spend)
- Peak and CTA: **"You decide"**, so creative direction is delegated

## Authored decisions
1. Vibe: an analog console in a dark room. Brushed off-black panels, engraved labels and amber lamps. References: a Studer tape machine faceplate, a Neve channel strip, and the app's own amber keycaps.
2. Journey: power on, the console, the meters, the sources, the take-away (export, library), the plans, power down.
3. Energy: quiet faceplate, then loud at the console, then measured through meters and sources, then calm at the close.
4. Feeling curve:
   - Faceplate: curiosity. The needles sit at rest, waiting for signal.
   - Console: **delight (peak)**. Pressing a key audibly changes the mix in your own headphones.
   - Meters: confidence. Live scopes read the same signal.
   - Sources: relief. A sync log shows the files arriving with nothing to babysit.
   - Export and library: ownership. The work leaves the app in the format you need.
   - Plans: clarity. One table, no tricks.
   - Close: resolve. Power down and the needles fall back to rest.
5. Peak: "You can actually press the Mono key on the website and hear your mix collapse." Lives in the Console act.
6. Tell-someone sentence: "It's the site where you play a mix through the app's console before you've even downloaded it."
7. Grammar: live surface. App and hardware chrome is the nav. Copy is written as labels and status lines.
8. Signature move: a real Web Audio signal chain. A synthesized stereo loop runs through working console modes into live VU needles and scopes. It starts only on a visitor's click and never autoplays.
- Authored silence: none.
